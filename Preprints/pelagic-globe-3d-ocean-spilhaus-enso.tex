\begin{abstract}
PelagicGlobe (v1.1.0) is an open-source, cross-platform ocean circulation and El Niño--Southern Oscillation (ENSO) workstation implemented in memory-safe Rust across core library modules under GPL-3.0-or-later licensing. Unlike conventional atmospheric map projections that bisect the Pacific or Arctic basins along land-centered meridians, PelagicGlobe unifies an interactive 3D orthographic ocean globe, an equirectangular 2D world ocean map, and a conformal Spilhaus World Ocean square projection (ESRI:54099) centered over Kerguelen (66.95°E, 49.56°S) with continental perimeter seams converging along the Bering Strait and Americas. The application embeds 921 seasonal Oceanic Niño Index (ONI) records spanning 1950--2026 (NOAA CPC ERSSTv6) paired with flavor-resolved spatial SST anomalies (Canonical Eastern Pacific, Central Pacific Modoki, NE Pacific Warm Blob, and La Niña Cold Tongue), a real-time 20°C equatorial thermocline cross-section (120°E to 80°W), 4,200 advected Lagrangian surface drifters across 14 boundary and equatorial currents, SQLite bookmark persistence, and background-threaded NOAA CoastWatch ERDDAP OISST v2.1 / NESDIS geostrophic raster ingestion. Across 12 compiled unit and CLI checks (12/12 passing), Spilhaus round-trip coordinates match analytic targets to 1e-4°, headless SVG export generates 797 current vectors and 65 continent polylines, Eastern Pacific 20°C thermocline depths shift from 16.0 m in the 1973 NDJ La Niña (-2.0°C) to 156.8 m in 1997 NDJ (+2.4°C), and live ERDDAP ingestion resolves 10,741 valid ocean grid cells for 2015-12-15 (stride 8).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Uninterrupted Ocean Cartography}
Cartographic projections centered over continental interiors fracture the interconnected world ocean along arbitrary antimeridian seams, obscures Antarctic Circumpolar connectivity, and fragments basin-scale ENSO teleconnections. PelagicGlobe v1.1.0 addresses this representational divide in standalone safe Rust by smoothly morphing between a 3D orthographic sphere, an equirectangular 2D world ocean map, and the conformal Spilhaus World Ocean square (ESRI:54099) anchored at Kerguelen Island (66.95 E, 49.56 S).

\section{Historical ENSO Flavor Architecture and Thermocline Physics}
Rather than scaling a single static warming dipole, PelagicGlobe classifies historical episodes from the embedded 1950-2026 NOAA CPC ERSSTv6 table (921 overlapping 3-month seasons) into Eastern Pacific Super El Nino (1982-83, 1997-98), Mixed Super + NE Pacific Warm Blob (2015-16), Central Pacific Modoki (2009-10), and La Nina Cold Tongue (1973-74, 1988-89, 2020-23) spatial modes, coupled dynamically to the zonal 20 C equatorial thermocline slope between 140 E and 85 W.

\section{CLI Validation Benchmarks and Live NOAA ERDDAP Ingestion}
Twelve automated Rust test targets pass without unsafe code in core libraries. Spilhaus forward-inverse round-trips across six ocean provinces match input longitude and latitude to four decimal places. In CLI benchmarks across historical episodes, the Eastern Pacific 20 C isotherm depth deepens from 16.0 m during the 1973 NDJ La Nina (-2.0 C) to 52.4 m in neutral 1960 DJF (+0.0 C), 85.4 m in the 2009 NDJ Modoki (+1.5 C), and 149.2-156.8 m across Super El Nino peaks (1997, 2015, 2026). Live ERDDAP retrieval for 2015-12-15 (stride 8) populates 10,741 valid ocean grid cells.
